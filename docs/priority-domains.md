# Research Priority Domains

Research and compare providers in these domains. Priority does not imply approval.

## 1. Automotive parts and vehicle identification
- VIN decoding and vehicle specification data.
- OEM and aftermarket part catalogs, fitment/interchange, supersessions.
- Regional availability, supplier catalogs, pricing, and stock.
- Global coverage, model-year depth, accuracy, update frequency, and commercial reuse rights are critical.

## 2. Suppliers and company intelligence
- Supplier discovery, company verification, business directories, product catalogs.
- Contact and sourcing data only where collection and use are lawful and terms permit it.

## 3. AI and language models
- Model APIs, embeddings, reranking, OCR/document extraction, speech, and vision.
- Compare free quotas, data retention/training terms, regional processing, latency, and predictable costs.

## 4. Search, web, and news
- Search APIs, news feeds, crawling/index access, and source attribution.
- Check caching, display, redistribution, and archival restrictions.

## 5. Finance, currencies, and markets
- FX rates, macroeconomic indicators, commodities, and market data.
- Distinguish delayed, indicative, and real-time data; assess redistribution and exchange licensing.

## 6. Government and statistics
- Official open-data portals, customs/trade statistics, regulatory datasets, and public registries.
- Prefer authoritative sources and document publication/update cadence.

## Comparison dimensions
Coverage and accuracy; official source quality; pricing and quotas; commercial rights; authentication; privacy/security; latency/reliability; documentation/SDK quality; test evidence; fallback alternatives.
